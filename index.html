Reels Studio

Reels Studio produces short animated comedy episodes with recurring AI-generated characters for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Every episode is written, generated and edited by an automated pipeline and reviewed by its owner before publishing. All videos are clearly labelled as AI-generated.

About the app

reelbot is a private tool used only by its owner to publish finished episodes to the owner's own Instagram and YouTube channels. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
